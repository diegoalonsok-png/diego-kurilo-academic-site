Biography

About the author

Architecture, independent scholarship and writing.

Diego Kurilo (Buenos Aires, 1973) is an Argentine-Spanish architect, urban planner, writer and independent researcher.

Areas of work

Research interests

Classical architectural theory · Vitruvian studies · Architectural symbolism · Ancient Persia · Egyptian history · Early Christianity

He graduated from Universidad de Flores (UFLO) in 2008 and founded Diego Kurilo & Partners Architects in 2002. His work examines the transmission of architectural ideas, construction traditions and the textual and material cultures of antiquity.

His academic publications include Byblos: Symbolic Architecture, The Architect of Theory, Persia and Ancient Judaism and Early Christianity: History, Texts and Material Culture of Christian Origins.

His literary work includes The End of Entropy and other philosophical fiction. He is associated with Sophia Lux Academic Editions and works in Buenos Aires.
